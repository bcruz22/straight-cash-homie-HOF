2027 keeper options

Who each manager can keep for the 2027 draft and which pick it costs, from current Sleeper rosters. Rosters change until the keeper deadline, so check again before you submit.

League keeper rules

  • Up to 2 keepers per team, and not at the same position.
  • A player can be kept for at most 2 straight seasons, no matter whose roster he was on. Drafted in 2019, kept in 2020 and 2021, back in the pool for the 2022 draft.
  • You lose the pick in the round your keeper was drafted. It has to be your own original pick in that round, not one you got in a trade.
  • A keeper who was a free agent or waiver pickup costs your 12th round pick.
  • A player who was drafted, dropped and picked up again stays tied to the round he was drafted in, not the 12th.

How these numbers are worked out
  • All data comes from the league's Sleeper history, following each season back to the one before it, starting in 2020.
  • This season counts only completed weeks. It adds to all-time records and the current standings, but not to titles, finishes or season records.
  • Places 1 to 6 come from the playoff bracket. Places 7 to 10 follow regular season rank. Last place means the worst regular season record.
  • Sleeper didn't record owners for two teams in 2021. They're credited to the managers who ran those teams in 2020 (Denny and Richie), which the commissioner's keeper tracker confirms.
  • Managers appear under their first names. Denny and Richie left after 2021.
  • 2020 keepers come from the commissioner's tracker, since Sleeper didn't flag keepers that year. Later years use Sleeper's keeper flags, with the commissioner's corrections: in 2024 each team carried over only one keeper where the tracker shows one, and players sent back to the draft and redrafted (Christian McCaffrey in 2021, Jonathan Taylor in 2023) count as draft picks, not keepers.
  • Starter points only count weeks a player was in that manager's starting lineup.
  • 2027 keeper costs use the round a player went in the 2026 draft, as a pick or a keeper. Anyone not taken in the 2026 draft counts as a pickup at round 12. The 2-season limit counts keeper years in a row on any roster, using Sleeper's keeper flags back to 2021.
